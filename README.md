# Bekam?

**Know the fair price before you pay**

**BINF 301 Information & Communication Architecture, GIU**
Team leader: Logyne Hegazi (17005452)

**Bekam?** is a web app for tourists in Egypt. It tells them what things should cost, so they can check a price before they pay, convert prices with live exchange rates, plan their day around the weather, budget their trip, learn key Arabic phrases, avoid common scams and tip fairly.

## The problem

Tourists don't know local prices, pay in an unfamiliar currency and can't speak Arabic. This makes them easy targets for inflated taxi fares, souvenir markups and staged scams. Existing travel apps list attractions and hotels, but none tells you whether the price someone just quoted you is fair.

**Target users:** first time international tourists in Cairo, Giza, Luxor and Aswan, especially independent travellers who are not on a guided tour.

## User stories (summary)

* **F1:** check whether a quoted price is fair, and browse fair prices by category
* **F2:** convert an EGP price into my currency, and swap the conversion direction
* **F3:** see today's weather for my city, and get indoor or outdoor suggestions based on the heat
* **F4:** pick sites and see the total ticket cost, and see how long my plan takes
* **F5:** find phrases by situation, and search the phrasebook
* **F6:** read common scams in my city, and report a scam I experienced
* **F7:** know how much to tip for a service, and split the bill and tip among my group
